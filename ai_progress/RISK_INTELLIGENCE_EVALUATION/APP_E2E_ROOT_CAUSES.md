## Reading
The architecture limits quality in three layers: (1) the paraphrase-only second stage plus the old "open session / please supply" framing pushes the model to talk about gaps instead of answering; (2) the safety checks were written for the electrical domain and misfire elsewhere (M7 number/approval checks), turning good answers into templates; (3) the structured-JSON reasoning route loses simple calculations. All three are absent in Arm D (single free-form call). This supports replacing the generic-focus path by the D route and keeping M7 only for claim-level checks with the electrical number check restricted to the electrical focus.
